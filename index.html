Worried it is not for you? It is.

Most people who walk in for the first time say the same things. Here is what actually happens.

"I have never done kickboxing"

Nobody expects you to. You learn a few simple punches and kicks, then we build them into the workout.

"I am not as fit as I used to be"

You work at your own pace. Take a breather whenever you like. Fitness comes week by week.

"My knees or back play up"

Tell the coach before class. Every exercise has a gentler option, and high kicks are always optional.

"I do not want to get hit"

You will not. It is completely non-contact. You hit pads and bags, never people.

Questions people ask

What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable gym clothes, trainers and a bottle of water. [Gloves and pads are provided / bring your own.]

Am I too old or unfit to start?

No. Our members range from their 20s to their 50s, and many started with no exercise background at all.

I have an injury or health condition. Can I still join?

Usually yes. Let the coach know before your first class so he can adapt the moves.

Will I have to spar or get hit?

Never. The classes are 100% non-contact.

Can I come with a friend or my partner?

Please do. Lots of members train in pairs. You can both book a free trial.
